## World Forge

**In development**

Developing a framework that turns books, films and shows into playable 3D worlds where players can change the story and build their own future. World-development logic, persistent state and character memory are intended to let the setting respond to player choices while keeping its lore, geography and characters coherent.

The first game focuses on one defined setting. The broader story-to-world framework remains a development goal. Engine code, unpublished lore and private implementation details remain private.

[Public project overview](https://github.com/scolety1/worldforge-showcase)

## Thousand Sunny Fleet + Dot

**In development and used in my own workflow**

A conversational command center for AI development teams, with captains coordinating parallel specialists across research, implementation, testing and independent review. Persistent project memory, recoverable handoffs, failure alerts and model/resource controls are central to the design.

I use Dot to direct work through TSF and Orca. The project-specific overlay builds on upstream Orca; it is not a wholly original IDE. The public source and dated component tests show specific parts of the system. Reliable unattended operation and complete integration remain active engineering work.

[Engineering tour](https://github.com/scolety1/orca/blob/tsf/main/tsf/SHOWCASE.md) · [TSF overlay source](https://github.com/scolety1/orca/tree/tsf/main/tsf)

## Niners War Room

**Local application in regular use**

A statistics-driven fantasy-football application I use to evaluate players and make roster decisions using football performance data and league context. I define the ranking approach, keeper/drop criteria and recommendation explanations, combining my own implementation with AI assistance.

The working local application is distinct from the unfinished API work. Broader goals include prioritized lineup, waiver, trade and draft actions; each model and workflow has its own scope rather than a blanket predictive-accuracy claim.

[Source and workflow guide](https://github.com/scolety1/Niners-War-Room)

## Urban Kitchen Beverage Menu / HouseOS

**Beverage menu shipped; HouseOS in final debugging**

The Urban Kitchen QR menu covers wine, whiskey and cocktails. Recommendations use deterministic rules. Restaurant management has completed the sign-in, Google Sheets/Drive editing and publishing workflow.

HouseOS is the broader front-of-house platform for training, staff communication, shift pickup/drop, tip-out workflows and management. I am completing its final debugging. The shipped guest menu is one component of that restaurant work, not a claim that the entire HouseOS platform has rolled out.

The public guest-site repository preserves an earlier CSV publication path. Private manager access and restaurant records are separate.

[Live guest menu](https://urbankitchenbeverage.com/) · [Guest-site source](https://github.com/scolety1/Urban-Kitchen-Wine-List)
